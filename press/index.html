short description

Farmion is a cozy voxel farming and life sim for PC. Grow crops, keep livestock and build a village with markets, shops and restaurants, alone or with friends in online co-op. Your farm keeps going while you're offline.

long description

Farmion is a cozy voxel farming and life sim game by Baer & Hoggo Games, a two-person studio. Shape the land, plant crops, trees and flowers, and keep livestock through the seasons, with rain, snow and wind. Greenhouses let you grow all year round.

Around the farm you build a village. Markets sell your produce over time, processors refine it into new goods and stockpiles grow your storage. Open a café, buffet or restaurant, a grocery store or a flower shop, each with its own skill tree, and let FarmBot drones help look after the fields.

Play solo or with friends in online co-op. Everyone on a farm shares its farm level, while your own progress stays with you. Crops keep growing, seasons keep turning and markets keep selling while you're offline.

Farmion is planned to enter Early Access in Q4 2026.

features

farm through the seasons

Shape the land and plant crops, trees and flowers. Keep livestock and look after them. Rain, snow and wind change the farm through the year, and greenhouses let you grow in any season.

build a village

Markets sell your produce over time, processors refine it into new goods and stockpiles add storage. Nicer surroundings make buildings better, so a pretty market holds more stock.

run your own businesses

Cook dishes in a café, buffet or restaurant for the big money, sell raw produce fast in a grocery store, or arrange bouquets in a flower shop to earn prestige. Each business has its own skill tree.

let the robots help

Set up FarmBot drones to tend your fields, so you can spend your time where you want it.

make it home

Build and furnish your own house, then decorate the farm with roads, lights, fences, decorations and pets.

do it together

Play solo or with friends in online co-op. The farm level decides what everyone can build, quests push the farm forward, and your own progress extends your tractor's reach. The farm keeps going while you're offline.
