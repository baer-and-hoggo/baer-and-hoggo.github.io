Farming

Build and farm.

Shape the terrain and place crops, trees, and flowers. Choose where your fields, paths, and buildings go.

Businesses

Run shops and restaurants.

Turn your harvest into new products, stock a grocery store, arrange flowers, or use your own ingredients in a café or restaurant.

Process goods

Combine ingredients into products for sale or use.

Run a shop

Stock the shelves and serve customers.

How you play.

01

Play solo or together.

Play on your own or with friends in online co-op.

Your character progress stays with you, including cosmetics and small unlocks such as harvesting larger areas.

02

Build, work, and trade.

Build villages, work in shops and restaurants, process goods, and sell what you make. Farm progress belongs to the save.

03

Progress while away.

Crops grow, customers shop, and processors keep working while you’re away.
